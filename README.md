PecPals is an Android application that revolutionises your approach to fitness through gamifying and simplifying the journey to a healthier, stronger you. When trying to establish a consistent exercise routine, many face hurdles that come with getting started – from the initial push to go to the gym, to navigating the overwhelming sea of workout guides, to overcoming the fear of judgement, and sticking to a regular workout regimen. 

PecPals has got you covered, tailored specifically for women as they don't already have a fitness community, users are invited to embark on a unique fitness journey, nurturing an egg into their very own PecPal! By engaging in exercise tasks featured within the app, users can foster growth and evolution in their little companions, blending the thrill of achievement with the joy of care and progress.

Moreover, PecPals leverages various design patterns such as Observer and Singleton design patterns to enhance user experience and streamline the application's functionality. Additionally, our Exercise Planner Algorithm generates tailor-made exercise plans that are specific to the user’s level and progress.
